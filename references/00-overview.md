# Buffett investing method

> This note records Warren Buffett's **stated** investing method, in his own words from Berkshire Hathaway's publications. It is not validated trading performance: nothing here has been back-tested or measured.

## Main idea

Buffett says he analyses a stock as if buying the whole business. He looks for a business he understands, with durable economics protected by a moat, run by able and honest managers, bought at a price comfortably below his estimate of intrinsic value, and held for the long term.

## Why it matters

A reference for evaluating companies the way Buffett describes. Each linked concept cites exact passages.

## Explanation or example

1. [Buffett circle of competence](01-circle-of-competence.md): stay where you can evaluate the business.
2. [Buffett business-quality checklist](02-business-quality-checklist.md): understandable, favourable long-term prospects, honest and competent people, attractive price.
3. [Buffett moats and durable competitive advantage](03-moats-and-durable-advantage.md): an enduring moat protecting high returns on capital, in a stable industry.
4. [Buffett return on equity and capital allocation](04-roe-and-capital-allocation.md): return on equity, not EPS growth; retained earnings must earn their keep.
5. [Buffett management quality](05-management-quality.md): able and honest managers, but business economics come first.
6. [Buffett owner earnings and intrinsic value](06-owner-earnings-and-intrinsic-value.md): value is discounted future cash; use owner earnings.
7. [Buffett margin of safety and Mr. Market](07-margin-of-safety-and-mr-market.md): buy only with a margin of safety; use market prices, don't follow them.
8. [Buffett lessons from his mistakes](08-lessons-from-mistakes.md): from cigar butts to wonderful businesses at fair prices.
